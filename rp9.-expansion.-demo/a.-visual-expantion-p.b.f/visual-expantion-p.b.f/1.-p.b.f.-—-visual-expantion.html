META-ABSTRACT

Abstract

This document provides the complete visual presentation of the established P.B.F. functional and relational structure across linear, bidirectional, planar, quadratic, cubic, scale-based, positional, and Rx8 reference fields.

Its function is to make the continuity of the established structure visually inspectable while direction, dimension, field size, scale, grouping, and representation change.

The document begins from the established P.B.F. differentiation and follows its visual representation through increasingly expanded fields. The same positional relations are thereby made readable through multiple geometric and proportional forms without treating the multiplication of directions, dimensions, scales, fields, or simultaneous readings as the generation of additional primary functions.

The visual sequence subsequently develops the circle-square construction, the invariant radial reference 𝑅, the complete linear enclosure 8𝑅, and the Rx8 relational reference field through which multiple established positions can be displayed and compared simultaneously.

The governing distinction throughout the document is:

ESTABLISHED P.B.F. FUNCTIONAL BASIS↓MULTIPLE VISUAL REPRESENTATIONS↓PRESERVED FUNCTIONAL IDENTITY

Geometry, direction, dimension, scale, and visual representation provide expressions of the established relations. They do not replace their functional basis.

1. VISUAL READING BASIS

The visual material begins from the established P.B.F. differentiation:

𝐼↓𝐼2+𝐼2↓𝐼4+𝐼4+𝐼2

1↓12+12↓14+14+12

Therefore:

𝑋−𝑋−𝑌=14−14−12.

The symbols 𝑋 and 𝑌 preserve the positional distinction while the visual field changes.

They do not replace the exact rational values.

The governing visual relation is:

ESTABLISHED FUNCTION↓DIRECTIONAL / GEOMETRIC REPRESENTATION
